How to choose a call blocker app

  • Caller identification: does the app tell you who's calling?
  • Automatic spam blocking: does it block junk calls without you lifting a finger?
  • Database: is the scam-number data updated often and relevant to what's happening in Vietnam?
  • Security and privacy: what permissions does the app ask for, and does it send your data to its servers?
  • Offline use: does it need an Internet connection to block calls?
  • Cost and language.

The 5 popular call blocker apps in detail

1. Truecaller

  • Pros: excellent at identifying unknown callers thanks to its huge user community; can block spam automatically.
  • Cons: Truecaller builds its database of names and numbers largely from its user community and asks for access to your contacts, which has raised plenty of privacy concerns — your name and number may appear on Truecaller even if you've never used the app (Truecaller says it doesn't upload users' entire contact lists to its servers and lets you remove your number from its public directory). The app also needs an Internet connection to look up phone numbers on its servers.
  • Cost: free version with ads; paid plans cost roughly 2,500,000–6,000,000 VND per year (approximate).

It's arguably the most popular call blocker today, and it's quite effective at identifying and blocking telemarketing and scam calls.

2. Hiya

Hiya has spam call detection, but it focuses more on businesses: it shows business names under phone numbers and helps companies avoid being flagged as spam when they need to call many customers. If you're an individual who just wants a call screening app for yourself, Hiya probably isn't the best fit.

3. Calls Blacklist

  • Pros: lets you build your own block list; works without the Internet.
  • Cons: no scam-number database built for Vietnam — you have to add each number or pattern yourself, so it's hard to keep up with scammers who keep switching numbers.

4. Should I Answer?

  • Pros: rates phone numbers based on community reports; can work offline.
  • Cons: developed in the Czech Republic, so its database doesn't closely reflect the scam landscape in Vietnam.

5. SafePhone — an anti-scam app built for Vietnam

  • Pros:
    • Developed in Vietnam; its scam-number list is compiled from multiple sources, including reports from its user community based on what's happening in Vietnam — see how SafePhone works.
    • The blacklist is built into the app, so it works even without the Internet.
    • Lets you create your own block rules by prefix, suffix, or number pattern — see how to block calls by number prefix or pattern.
    • Offers a mode to block calls from numbers not in contacts, with a built-in trusted list of bank and government agency numbers.
    • Blacklist blocking doesn't need access to your contacts; only the contacts-only calling mode needs that permission, to import your saved numbers.
    • Beyond call blocking, SafePhone also blocks scam websites, suspicious links, and fake links.
    • If you're the most tech-savvy person in your family, SafePhone can alert you when a family member receives a call from an unknown number or taps a suspicious link — see protect your family from scam calls.
  • Cons: no iPhone version yet, because iOS doesn't let other apps take over the iPhone's built-in call blocking the way Android does.
  • Cost: free.

Blocking calls without an app: Android's built-in features

Before installing an app, you can use the built-in features (menu names may vary by manufacturer and version):

  • Block individual numbers: open your call log, long-press the number → Block.
  • Block private and unknown numbers: Phone app → Settings → Blocked numbers.
  • Filter spam calls: Google's Phone app has a Caller ID & spam setting; Samsung phones have a similar option in call settings.

These features are useful, but they only block numbers you already know or widespread spam, and they have no data specific to scams in Vietnam. For the full walkthrough, see how to block unknown and scam numbers automatically.

Why are call blocker apps limited on iPhone?

On Android, an app granted the call screening permission can check the incoming number before the phone rings and block it using its own rules. iOS restricts this at the system level, so automatic blocking features like SafePhone's can't fully work on iPhone. iPhone users can turn on Silence Unknown Callers under Settings → Phone to send calls from numbers not in their contacts to voicemail.

Beyond calls, remember that scammers also send text messages with links — see how to check if a website is a scam.

FAQ

What is the best call blocker app for users in Vietnam?

It depends on your needs. Truecaller is strong at showing caller names. SafePhone is free, works without an Internet connection, and has a scam-number list compiled for the situation in Vietnam, plus prefix blocking and a contacts-only calling mode.

Is Truecaller safe?

Truecaller is popular and good at identifying unknown numbers, but its database is community-sourced and the app needs access to your contacts, so many people have privacy concerns. You can read Truecaller's policy and request that your number be removed from its public directory.

Can I install several call blocker apps at once?

Android only lets one app hold the call screening role at a time, so installing several apps won't block more calls and can cause conflicts. Pick one main call blocker.

Do call blocker apps work without an Internet connection?

It depends on the app. Truecaller needs the Internet to look up numbers. SafePhone, Calls Blacklist, and Should I Answer? can block calls without an Internet connection.

Is there an iPhone version of SafePhone?

Not yet. iOS doesn't let other apps take over the built-in call blocking the way Android does, so SafePhone is currently available on Android only.
