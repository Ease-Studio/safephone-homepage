Privacy Policy - SafePhone

The SafePhone application is committed to protecting users' privacy and personal data. This document explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use the application.

1. Information we collect

SafePhone may collect certain types of information in order to provide and improve the service:

  • Device information (model, operating system, application version)
  • List of accessed URLs (used only for safety checks)
  • Incoming phone numbers (to detect spam/scams, if the user grants permission)
  • Activity logs to improve the experience

2. How we use information

Information is used for the following purposes:

  1. Detecting and blocking malicious or fraudulent websites
  2. Warning of calls from dangerous phone numbers
  3. Improving security algorithms and the user experience
  4. Ensuring the application operates stably

3. Access permissions and system permissions

SafePhone may request certain important permissions:

  • Call Screening: to detect and warn of scam phone numbers
  • Internet: to check the safety of websites
  • Overlay / Accessibility (if any): to display security warnings

We use these permissions only for security purposes and do not use them for any improper purpose.

4. Data sharing

SafePhone does not sell, trade or share users' personal data with third parties, except:

  • When required by law
  • To protect the rights, interests and safety of users

5. Data storage and security

Data is stored securely and only for as long as necessary to serve the application's functions. We apply security measures to prevent unauthorized access.

6. User rights

Users have the right to:

  • Control and grant/revoke access permissions
  • Request the deletion of personal data
  • Stop using the application at any time

7. Third-party services

The application may use third-party services (e.g., analytics, crash reporting). These services have their own privacy policies.

8. Children

SafePhone does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 years of age. If such data is discovered, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Users should check it periodically to stay informed of any changes.
