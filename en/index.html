SafePhone's anti-scam features

SafePhone combines constantly updated lists of scam phone numbers and domains with analysis of impersonation signals, so it protects you even when scammers switch numbers or domains.

Block calls from unknown and scam numbers

Automatically blocks spam calls, robocalls and numbers showing signs of fraud before your phone rings. You can accept calls only from your contacts, or block by prefix, ending digits and number patterns — especially useful for protecting older adults.

SafePhone FAQ

How does SafePhone block scam calls?

SafePhone uses Android's Call Screening permission to check incoming numbers before your phone rings. You can block numbers on a scam list, only accept calls from your contacts, or block by number prefix and number patterns.

How can I check whether a link is a scam?

When you set SafePhone as your default browser, every link you tap is checked against a list of scam websites and analyzed for signs of impersonation before it opens. Dangerous links are blocked and you get a warning.

Does SafePhone work on iPhone?

SafePhone is currently available on Google Play for Android phones. iOS restricts call and link screening at the system level, so the automatic blocking features can't work fully on iPhone. Learn more.

How will I know when my parents or kids get a call from an unknown number?

SafePhone can send an alert to a shared family channel whenever a family member gets a call from an unknown number or opens a suspicious link, so you can call them back and help in time.

Do I have to turn on every feature?

No. SafePhone is modular: just turn on the features you need, for example only blocking unknown numbers for your parents, or only blocking harmful websites for your kids.
